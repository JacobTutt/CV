\cvsection{Summary}\par
\vspace{1.90mm}
\begin{cvparagraph}
PhD researcher in machine learning and computational statistics at the Cavendish Laboratory and Kavli Institute for Cosmology, University of Cambridge. My research looks at how the latest statistical and probabilistic machine learning methods, combined with distributed computing, can tackle previously intractable inference problems in physics. Primarily, I am focused on the statistical detection and characterisation of the cosmological 21-cm signal, but am more broadly motivated by the challenge of scaling statistical and numerical methods, both algorithmically and computationally, to ever larger datasets, parameter spaces and physical systems.
\end{cvparagraph}
